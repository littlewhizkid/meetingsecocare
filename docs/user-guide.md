# ecoCare Meeting Room Booking — User Guide

**URL:** https://meeting.ecocare.id

---

## 1. Sign In

1. Open your browser and go to **https://meeting.ecocare.id**
2. Enter your **work email address** (e.g. `yourname@ecocare.co.id`)
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click **Sign In**

> If you have never logged in before or forgot your password, contact your system administrator to reset it.

---

## 2. The Booking Calendar

After signing in you will see the main calendar. It shows all four meeting rooms side by side for a selected date:

| Room | Capacity | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| 🏛️ Board Room | 12 people | Large meetings, presentations |
| 💼 Small Meeting Room | 6 people | Team discussions, 1-on-1s |
| 🎙️ Podcast Room | 10 people | Recordings, training sessions |
| 🤝 Interview Room | 4 people | Interviews, private meetings |

Each column shows the time slots for that room. **Green slots** are available, **filled slots** are already booked.

---

## 3. Navigate to a Different Date

- Click the **left arrow (←)** to go to the previous day
- Click the **right arrow (→)** to go to the next day
- Click the **date itself** to open a calendar picker and jump directly to any date

---

## 4. Book a Meeting Room

1. Find the room and time slot you want
2. **Click on an empty slot** — a booking form will appear
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Start Time** — pre-filled from the slot you clicked (you can change it)
   - **End Time** — select when your meeting ends
   - **Your Name** — pre-filled with your account name
   - **Meeting Title** — enter a short description of the meeting
4. Click **Confirm Booking**
5. You will see a green confirmation message at the top of the screen

> **Note:** You cannot book a slot that is in the past.

---

## 5. View Your Bookings

1. Click the **My Bookings** button in the top-right corner of the screen
2. A panel will slide open showing all your upcoming bookings
3. Each booking shows the room name, date, time, and meeting title

---

## 6. Cancel a Booking

**From the calendar:**
1. Find your booking on the calendar (shown in green)
2. Hover over it — an **✕** button will appear in the top-right corner of the booking
3. Click **✕**
4. A confirmation prompt will appear — click **Yes, Cancel Booking** to confirm

**From My Bookings:**
1. Open **My Bookings** (top-right button)
2. Find the booking you want to cancel
3. Click **Cancel** next to it
4. Confirm when prompted

> **Note:** You can only cancel your own bookings. Admins can cancel any booking.

---

## 7. Sign Out

Click the **sign-out icon** (→ arrow) next to your name in the top-right corner.

---

## Tips

- **Double-booked?** The system prevents overlapping bookings for the same room automatically.
- **Need a longer slot?** Select the start time first, then choose an end time that covers the full duration.
- **Past slots** are shown faded and cannot be clicked.
- **All times** are in Western Indonesia Time (WIB, UTC+7).
